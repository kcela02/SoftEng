4.6 EVALUATION SUMMARY
================================================================================

This section summarizes the overall results of the system testing and evaluation.


4.6.1 System Strengths
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Multi-horizon forecasting (1-day, 7-day, 30-day) provides granular restocking guidance
- Automatic ARIMA order selection via AIC ensures optimal model configuration without manual tuning
- Intelligent multi-level restock alerts (CRITICAL, HIGH, MEDIUM) with recommended order quantities minimize both stockouts and overstock
- Role-based access control (Admin and Staff roles) with account lockout and CSRF protection ensures secure operations
- Cloud deployment on Render with PostgreSQL provides high availability and easy access from any browser


4.6.2 System Limitations
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Based on Section 1.6 (Scope and Limitations):

1. Forecast accuracy depends on data quality
   - ARIMA and Linear Regression models are trained exclusively on historical sales data. Incomplete, inconsistent, or insufficient CSV data (fewer than 30 days for ARIMA, fewer than 5 days for Linear Regression) will degrade forecast reliability.

2. Cannot account for external factors (market changes, regulations)
   - The system cannot incorporate events such as supplier shortages, regulatory changes on vape products, or sudden shifts in consumer demand driven by external market forces.

3. Single-case study (not generalizable to other businesses)
   - The system was designed and tested specifically for VAPE CRIB's product catalog and sales patterns. Adapting it to businesses in other industries would require reconfiguration of product categories, alert thresholds, and forecasting parameters.

4. Requires user adoption and willingness to use
   - The effectiveness of the system depends on regular use by VAPE CRIB staff. If staff bypass the system and reorder products based on habit rather than consulting alerts and forecasts, the benefits of data-driven inventory management will not be realized.


4.6.3 Overall Assessment
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Predictive Sales & Restocking Dashboard System has been successfully 
developed, deployed, and evaluated. The system meets all specified functional 
and non-functional requirements and provides significant value to VAPE CRIB 
by enabling data-driven inventory decisions, reducing the risk of stockouts 
through proactive forecasting-based alerts, and replacing manual guesswork 
with quantified demand predictions.
